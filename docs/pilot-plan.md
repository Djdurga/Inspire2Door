# Hypothetical Concierge Pilot

This plan is a proposal only. No customer research, merchant interviews, or pilot have been conducted by this repository.

## Question

Will shoppers use inspiration-image discovery to find in-stock products from independent boutiques nearby, and will merchants keep availability current or pay for the resulting orders?

## Small test

1. Choose one neighborhood and a narrow category, such as independent fashion boutiques.
2. Interview 10-20 shoppers and 5-10 merchants about how they currently discover, confirm, and purchase local items.
3. Recruit 3-5 merchants for a manually operated catalog with explicit stock confirmation.
4. Test with a simple page and manual WhatsApp coordination; do not build dispatch automation or claim instant delivery.
5. Track search-to-contact conversion, confirmed in-stock rate, completed orders, repeat intent, merchant time spent updating stock, and willingness to pay.

## Decision gate

Proceed only if shoppers complete meaningful purchases and merchants can maintain accurate availability at an acceptable effort. Stop or change category if stock confirmation fails or the pilot cannot acquire shoppers economically. Set thresholds with participants before the test; do not retrofit success criteria after seeing results.
